<div class="rich">
  <p class="lead">I build product strategy from power system constraints and market realities.</p>
  <p>My career spans technical project management, customer accounts, product marketing, and product line ownership at Qorvo and Texas Instruments. That experience shapes how I connect circuit-level decisions to customer value and portfolio direction.</p>
  <h2>How I work</h2>
  <p>I start with the system: its efficiency targets, thermal limits, reliability requirements, cost, and the team’s ability to implement it. Then I work through which constraints should drive the product decision.</p>
  <p>That often means looking beyond an individual device. Power architecture interacts with firmware, RF performance, and platform requirements. A roadmap has to reflect those relationships as well as customer needs and market timing.</p>
  <h2>Current focus</h2>
  <ul><li>RF biasing, PMIC, and DC-DC product roadmaps</li><li>Power architecture and integration decisions</li><li>System reliability and customer adoption</li><li>Semiconductor portfolio positioning</li></ul>
