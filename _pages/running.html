How I calculate it

I use steady aerobic laps near 145 bpm, convert COROS adjusted pace to speed, use the initial fitted heart-rate correction, and estimate each run at 145 bpm. Qualifying laps last at least 10 minutes, have an average heart rate of 140–150 bpm, and exclude the first lap. Corrected speeds are averaged by lap duration. Warmups, short recordings, threshold sessions, and travel or unusually hilly runs are excluded.

Calibration: corrected speed (m/s) = 1000 ÷ adjusted pace (sec/km) − 0.0116511339 × (lap heart rate − 145). Estimated pace (sec/mile) = 1609.344 ÷ duration-weighted corrected speed. The trend is an unweighted linear fit of run pace against date, scaled to 30.44 days; it is not a forecast.

Adjusted pace estimates equivalent flat-ground pace. Weather, fatigue, cardiac drift, and terrain can still move an individual point, so the multi-week trend matters more than any single run.
