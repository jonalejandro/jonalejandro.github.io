Interpretation
Four qualifying steady aerobic laps; grade, climate, and heart rate normalized to 145 bpm.
View the full qualification rules and method

Steady aerobic laps must last at least 10 minutes, average 140–150 bpm, and cannot be the first lap. Warmups, short recordings, threshold sessions, trail runs, indoor runs, and altitude or travel runs are excluded.

Heart rate is normalized to 145 bpm using the initial fitted correction. Grade uses the Minetti energy-cost curve. Climate estimates use temperature, dew point, humidity, and modeled wind exposure. Corrected speeds are averaged by qualifying-lap duration.

The recent trend is an unweighted linear fit of included pace against date, scaled to 30.44 days. It is descriptive, not a forecast. Sleep, fatigue, fueling, cardiac drift, surface, and weather-station distance can still move a point.
